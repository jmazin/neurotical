Stage 7 · Hidden layer

The perfect toast

Too low and the bread is raw. Too high and it's burnt. Only the middle of the dial makes perfect toast.

One neuron can only draw one cutoff. It can learn “higher is better” or “lower is better”, but never “just right”. So we give the network a hidden layer of two neurons, and let it work out the rest from examples.

  1. Press learn and watch the curve grow a bump over the perfect settings.
  2. Turn on show hidden to see each hidden neuron's own S curve.
  3. Hover over the plot to send a toaster setting through the network beside it. Click to pin a setting, and double-click to let it go.
  4. Drag the sliders to set the weights and biases by hand.
  5. Press reset for a new random start. Now and then it gets stuck, and that's worth seeing too.
