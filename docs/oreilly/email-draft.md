Hello O’Reilly team,

I’d like to propose a two-hour beginner live course, **Neural Networks You Can See: Build intuition through interactive experiments, from one neuron to a small classifier**.

The course is for developers who can read simple code but are new to machine learning. It uses interactive browser demos and short JavaScript examples to make the calculations visible. Learners start by moving an exam pass/fail cutoff by hand, then measure loss and implement a small training step. They progressively introduce weight, sigmoid activation, two inputs, and a hidden layer.

Each lesson asks learners to predict a result, change one thing, and explain what happened. By the end, they can implement and train a single neuron, distinguish loss from accuracy, and explain why some patterns require more than one linear decision boundary.

I’m a software engineer and technical educator with more than 20 years of software-development experience, a former Lead Educator at BrainStation, and the creator of Learning Twice. I also develop and deliver curriculum as a contract instructor at Toronto Metropolitan University. I’m developing Neurotical, the browser-based project behind these exercises.
